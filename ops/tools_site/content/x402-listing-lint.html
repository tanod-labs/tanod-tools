    The rules this checks
    

    The Coinbase CDP x402 Bazaar lists paid endpoints for agents to discover. Listing quality decides whether an agent can pick your endpoint without guessing. This tool checks the pieces you control in the 402 response.
    

      
  
    • Description must be present and at most 500 characters. CDP rejects longer ones at settle time.
      • 
  
    • A "when to use" sentence is a curation rule. In our 2026-10-09 snapshot of 34,062 Bazaar listings, 94.4% lack one, 103 are over 500 characters and 91 are empty.
      • 
  
    • Resource URL must be https. mimeType and outputSchema must be present.
      • 
  
    • Each payment option needs a positive integer amount, a network, an asset and a payTo address.
      • 

    

    The full list, with the free validate call and the featured-tier criteria, is in How to get an x402 endpoint listed and featured in the CDP Bazaar. For payer counts by endpoint, see what agents pay for over x402.
    

    To check listings from code or CI, use the x402 listing lint API.
