    PNG screenshots and scans as one PDF

    Screenshots of a receipt, a conversation or a set of slides are easier to send, archive and print as a single PDF than as a folder of images. PNG is the format most screenshot tools save in, and because it is lossless, PDF can carry it without any change: the pixels are compressed with the same kind of lossless method and placed on the page.

    The order of the list is the order of the pages. Long screenshots become long pages with Fit to image; with a paper size they are scaled down to fit, so very tall captures may print small and are best split first.

    Tips

    • Adding the result to an existing document? Use Merge PDF.
    • Got the order wrong after saving? Fix it with Reorder PDF pages.
    • Turning photos into a PDF? JPG to PDF keeps JPG files compact.
