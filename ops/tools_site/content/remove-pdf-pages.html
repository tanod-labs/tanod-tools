Deleting pages without re-printing the PDF

Blank scanner pages, an outdated appendix, a cover sheet nobody needs: removing them is a matter of writing a new page list that skips them. The pages you keep are copied as they are, so nothing is re-rendered and nothing loses quality.

Pages marked for removal are faded and struck through in the grid, so you can check the result before you save.

Tips

  • Keeping only a few pages out of many? Extract pages is the same job from the other side.
  • Pages in the wrong order or sideways? Fix them with Reorder pages and Rotate PDF.
