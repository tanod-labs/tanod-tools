Density altitude

The formulas

The calculation uses the International Standard Atmosphere (ICAO Doc 7488, ISO 2533): sea-level temperature T0 = 288.15 K, pressure P0 = 1013.25 hPa, density 1.225 kg/m³, a lapse rate L = 0.0065 K/m up to 11,000 m, and then a constant 216.65 K. The pressure at altitude h in the troposphere is P = P0 · (1 − L·h/T0)^(g/(R·L)), where g = 9.80665 m/s², R = 287.05287 J/(kg·K) and the exponent is 5.25588.

Station pressure is found from the altimeter setting as P_station = QNH · (1 − L·elevation/T0)^5.25588. The pressure altitude is the ISA altitude with that pressure: h = (T0/L) · (1 − (P_station/P0)^(1/5.25588)). Air density is ρ = P_station / (R · T) from the ideal gas law with the outside air temperature in kelvin, and the density altitude is the ISA altitude with that density: h = (T0/L) · (1 − (ρ/ρ0)^(1/4.25588)). Above 11 km the isothermal layer is used instead. The temperature deviation is the OAT minus the ISA temperature at the pressure altitude.

Conversions are exact (1 ft = 0.3048 m, 1 inHg = 3,386.38864 Pa). Not for flight planning or navigation.
