    Vietnam's electronic invoices
    

    Since the Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC, businesses in Vietnam issue hóa đơn điện tử, electronic invoices, as XML files in a format set by the tax authority. An invoice either carries a code from the tax authority (hóa đơn có mã, shown in the element MCCQT) or is issued without one. The XML is the invoice; the readable version is a drawing of it. This page makes that drawing in your browser from the XML you have.
    

    What the viewer shows
    

    You see the invoice name and version, the form number (KHMSHDon), the symbol (KHHDon), the number (SHDon), the date (NLap), the tax authority's code (MCCQT) when there is one, the seller (NBan) and the buyer (NMua) with their tax codes (MST), addresses and contacts, every line with unit, quantity, price, discount and VAT rate, VAT by rate (THTTLTSuat), the totals, the amount in words and the payment details. For an adjusting or replacing invoice (TCHDon), the invoice it refers to is shown. Amounts are shown exactly as in the file.
    

    It does not verify the digital signatures (DSCKS), does not validate the invoice against the tax authority's schema and rules and does not look the invoice up. Invoices in other formats, such as the older invoices of Circular 32/2011, are not read.
