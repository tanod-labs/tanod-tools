Pulling pages out of a PDF

Extracting copies the pages you choose into a fresh PDF and leaves everything else behind. It is the quickest way to send someone one section of a long report, keep only the signed pages of a contract, or drop the cover and appendix before printing.

The thumbnails are drawn on your device as you scroll, so even long documents open quickly. Typing ranges is faster for big selections; clicking is easier for a few scattered pages. Both stay in sync.

Tips

  • To cut a document into several parts in one go, use Split PDF.
  • To keep most pages and drop a few, Remove pages is quicker.
