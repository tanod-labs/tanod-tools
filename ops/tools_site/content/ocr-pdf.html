What OCR does to a scanned PDF

A scanner or phone app saves each page as a picture. You can read it, but your computer can't: search finds nothing and you can't copy a sentence. Optical character recognition (OCR) looks at the picture, finds the letters and words, and writes them into the PDF as invisible text placed exactly over the words you see.

Our server renders each chosen page at 200 to 300 dpi, reads it with the open-source Tesseract engine, and lays the text over the original page without changing it. The recognised text is also shown here, so you can copy it straight away.

Tips

  • Scan at 300 dpi in black and white or grey for the best accuracy.
  • Pages upside down or sideways? Turn them first with Rotate PDF (in your browser).
  • Got a photo or screenshot instead of a PDF? Use Image to text.
