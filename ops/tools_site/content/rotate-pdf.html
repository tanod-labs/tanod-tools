Rotation that stays put

Scanners and phone cameras often save pages sideways. A PDF reader can turn the view, but that setting usually isn't saved, so the next person sees the pages sideways again. This tool writes the rotation into the file.

Each PDF page carries a rotation value of 0, 90, 180 or 270 degrees. Changing it is instant and lossless: the text, images and drawings on the page stay exactly as they were.

Tips

  • Rotating a photo rather than a PDF? Use Rotate image.
  • Pages also out of order? Run the result through Reorder pages.
