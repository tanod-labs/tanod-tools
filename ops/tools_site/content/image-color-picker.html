      Reading colours straight from the pixels

      Matching a brand colour from a logo, finding the exact blue in a screenshot, or building a palette from a photo all come down to the same thing: reading the red, green and blue values stored for a pixel. This tool decodes the image in your browser and reads those values from the full-resolution picture, so the code you copy is the colour that is actually in the file.

      Single pixels can mislead in photos, where noise and compression make neighbours vary. Use the magnifier to aim at the middle of a flat area, or take the palette, which averages whole groups of similar pixels into a handful of representative colours.

      Tips

      • Convert a picked colour to CMYK or check its contrast against text with the Color converter.
      • Picking from a small part of a big image? Crop it first so the area fills the screen.
