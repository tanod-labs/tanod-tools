    Resizing images in your browser

    Resizing means working out a new grid of pixels from the old one. Shrinking averages groups of pixels together, which is why a good downscale looks smooth and a careless one looks jagged. This tool halves the image repeatedly while it is more than twice the target, then makes a final draw to the exact size with high-quality smoothing.

    The aspect ratio is the width divided by the height. Keeping it locked means a 1200 × 800 photo set to 600 pixels wide becomes 600 × 400, with no stretching. Turn the lock off only when you want an exact box and accept the distortion.
