Compression
Recommended suits most files. Strong gives the smallest file with softer photos. Lossless leaves images untouched.

How PDF compression works

Most of a large PDF's weight is pictures: scanned pages, photos, screenshots. Our server opens the file, finds every photo-like image and checks how sharp it is at the size it is printed on the page. Anything sharper than the level's limit is scaled down, then saved again as JPEG. An image is only replaced when that saves at least 10 percent, and the file's structure is rewritten with every stream compressed as tightly as possible.

The result is never bigger than your original. When nothing can be saved, you're told, and you keep the file you have.

Tips

  • For email, try Recommended first; switch to Strong only if the file is still too big.
  • Need only some pages? Take them out first with Extract pages; fewer pages means a smaller file. That tool runs in your browser.
  • Shrinking photos rather than a PDF? Compress image works on your device, without uploading.
