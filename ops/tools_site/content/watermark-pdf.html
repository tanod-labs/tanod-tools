Watermarking a PDF in your browser

A watermark is text drawn on each page. Your browser opens the PDF with pdf-lib, works out the visible area of every page, and draws the text there with the colour, opacity and angle you chose. The result is a new file; your original stays as it was.

The text uses Helvetica, one of the fonts every PDF viewer already has, which keeps the output small but limits the characters to the Western European Latin set. Tiled placement repeats the text in a staggered pattern so it can't be cropped away from one corner.

Tips

  • Keep the opacity low, around 20 to 35 percent, so the page stays readable.
  • Want page numbers as well? Add them with Add page numbers.
  • Need to share a flattened copy? Turn the pages into pictures with PDF to JPG.
