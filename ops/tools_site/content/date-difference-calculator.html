How the difference is counted

Total days is simply how many calendar days lie between the two dates. To split that into years, months and days, the calculator counts whole months forward from the start date for as long as it doesn't pass the end date, then counts the days left. A month that is too short for the start day ends on its last day, so 31 January plus one month is the end of February.

Business days are the Mondays to Fridays among the counted days. With Include the end date off, the range runs from the start date up to, but not including, the end date; switching it on adds the end date to both the total and the business day count.

Everything is worked out on the calendar rather than with clock time, so the result is the same whatever time zone your device is in and whether or not the range crosses a daylight saving change.
