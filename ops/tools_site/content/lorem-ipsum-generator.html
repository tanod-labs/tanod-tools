Placeholder text for layouts

Designers and developers use filler text to see how headings, columns and line lengths behave before the real copy exists. Lorem ipsum is popular because its word lengths and letter patterns look much like ordinary text, but it is not in any language the reader will try to read.

This generator draws words at random from the classic vocabulary and builds sentences and paragraphs of natural-looking length. Everything happens in your browser, so you can generate as much as you like.

Tips

  • Check how long the text is with the Word counter.
  • Change capitalisation with the Case converter.
