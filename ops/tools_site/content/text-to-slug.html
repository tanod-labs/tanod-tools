How the slug is made

Each line of text goes through the same steps. Accented letters are converted to their plain Latin form and Greek and Cyrillic letters are transliterated, so "Crème brûlée" becomes "creme-brulee" and "Москва" becomes "moskva". Apostrophes are removed without leaving a gap, so "don't" stays one word. Every other run of spaces and punctuation becomes a single separator, and separators at the start and end are trimmed.

The preview shows the slug as it would appear at the end of a web address. Keep slugs short and descriptive: a few words that say what the page is about are easier to read, share and remember than a full headline.

Changing a published page's slug changes its address, so links to the old address break unless the site redirects them. Pick the slug before you publish where you can.

Tips

  • Check a title's length before you turn it into a slug with the Character counter.
  • Need camelCase or snake_case names for code instead? Use the Case converter.
  • To put text with spaces and symbols into a URL as it is, encode it with URL encode and decode.
