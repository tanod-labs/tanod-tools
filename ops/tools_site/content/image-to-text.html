From picture to text

A photo of a page is just coloured dots to a computer. OCR, optical character recognition, finds the shapes of letters in those dots and turns them back into text you can search, edit and paste. This tool sends one prepared copy of your image to our server, where the Tesseract engine reads it, and shows you the words in seconds.

Tips for better results

  • Hold the phone straight above the page, in good light, and fill the frame with the text.
  • Crop away everything that isn't text with Crop image, and turn sideways photos with Rotate image. Both run in your browser.
  • Have a whole scanned document? OCR PDF makes the PDF itself searchable.
