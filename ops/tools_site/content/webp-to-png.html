    Why WebP images need converting

    WebP is a web format: it has a lossy mode for photos and a lossless mode for graphics, both with transparency, and it makes pages load faster. Outside the browser, support is patchier. A WebP saved from a shopping site or a news article may refuse to open in an older editor, attach to a form or print from a desktop app.

    This page decodes the WebP with your browser's own decoder, which already understands both modes and transparency, and writes the pixels out as PNG. Nothing is uploaded, so it also works for images you would rather not send to a conversion server.

    Tips

    • Converting a photo? JPG gives a far smaller file than PNG; switch the output above, or use PNG to JPG later.
    • Need the image as text for HTML or CSS instead? Use Image to Base64.
    • Other formats, including AVIF and BMP, are handled by Convert image.
