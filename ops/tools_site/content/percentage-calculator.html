The formulas behind each row

Every percentage question is a rearrangement of one relationship: part = percentage ÷ 100 × whole. Which of the three you are missing decides the sum.

  • Percentage of a number: part = p ÷ 100 × whole. 15% of 80 = 0.15 × 80 = 12.
  • What percent: p = part ÷ whole × 100. 12 is 12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15% of 80.
  • Find the whole: whole = part ÷ (p ÷ 100). If 12 is 15% of something, that something is 12 ÷ 0.15 = 80.
  • Percentage change: (new − old) ÷ |old| × 100. From 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = +25%.
  • Increase or decrease: new = old × (1 ± p ÷ 100). 80 increased by 15% is 80 × 1.15 = 92.

A common slip is to add and subtract percentages as if they were fixed amounts. A 10% discount followed by another 10% discount is a 19% discount in total, not 20%, because the second one applies to the already reduced price.
