Files to create

    Three ways to split

    Page ranges is for documents with a known structure: chapters, invoices bundled in one scan, or the signed pages of a contract. Write the ranges you want and each becomes its own file.

    Every N pages cuts at a fixed interval. It suits scans of double-sided forms (every 2 pages) or batches of identical documents printed into one PDF.

    Every page makes one PDF per page, which is useful before re-ordering pages by hand or sending them to different people.

    Tips

    • Want the chosen pages in a single file instead of several? Use Extract pages.
    • Need the pages as pictures? PDF to JPG turns each page into an image.
