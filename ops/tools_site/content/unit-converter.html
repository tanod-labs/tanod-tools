How the conversions work

Each category has a base unit: metres for length, kilograms for weight, litres for volume, square metres for area and metres per second for speed. A value is multiplied by its unit's size in the base unit, then divided by the size of the target unit. Because every factor is an exact definition (1 inch = 0.0254 m exactly, 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly), the result is as precise as the numbers you type.

Temperature works differently because the scales have different zero points. Celsius and Kelvin share a degree size but Kelvin starts at absolute zero, so K = °C + 273.15. Fahrenheit degrees are 5/9 the size of Celsius degrees and the scale is shifted, so °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. Rankine is the Fahrenheit-sized scale that starts at absolute zero.

Some units share a name but not a size. US and UK gallons, pints and fluid ounces differ, and so do US, UK and metric tons, so they are listed separately and labelled.
