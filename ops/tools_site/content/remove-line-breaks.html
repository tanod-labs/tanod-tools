Turning broken lines back into paragraphs

Text copied from PDFs, old emails, terminal windows and some websites often has a hard line break at the end of every visual line. Pasted into a document or a form, it stays broken into short lines that don't reflow. This tool removes those breaks and joins the pieces with a space, so the text flows again.

Keep paragraphs is the right choice for most prose: it keeps the blank lines that separate paragraphs and joins everything else. Remove all puts the whole text on one line, which suits short snippets, list items you want comma-separated, or values going into a single spreadsheet cell.

PDF text sometimes splits words across lines with a hyphen. Turn on Join words split by a hyphen to put them back together, and check the result for real hyphenated words.

Tips

  • Need to change other characters too, such as double spaces after full stops or curly quotes? Follow up with Find and replace.
  • Count the cleaned text with the Word counter or the Character counter.
  • Going the other way, from a list to sorted lines? Use Sort lines alphabetically.
