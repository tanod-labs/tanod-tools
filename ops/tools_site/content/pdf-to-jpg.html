Converting PDF pages to images in your browser

A PDF page is a list of drawing instructions, not a picture. To get an image, the page has to be drawn at a chosen size. Your browser does this with pdf.js onto a canvas at the resolution you pick, one page at a time, then saves the canvas as a JPG or PNG file.

Resolution is measured in dots per inch. A PDF page is defined in points, 72 to the inch, so 150 dpi draws each page a little over twice as wide as its nominal size. Choose JPG for photographs and smaller files, or PNG when you want crisp text and edges with no compression artefacts.

Tips

  • Going the other way? JPG to PDF builds a PDF from images.
  • Only need a few pages as a PDF rather than images? Use Extract pages.
  • Resize or recompress the images afterwards with Compress image.
