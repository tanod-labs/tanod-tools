How an exact age is counted

Years and months are counted the way people count birthdays: you are a year older on the same day and month as your birth, and a month older on the same day of each month. When that day doesn't exist (31 April, or 29 February in a common year), the last day of the month is used instead. Whatever is left after the last whole month is the days.

The totals are simpler. Total days is the number of calendar days from the birth date to the Age on date, total weeks is that divided by seven and rounded down, and total months is the years times twelve plus the months.

The calculation uses calendar dates only, never clock time, so a daylight saving change or a time zone difference cannot shift the answer by a day. The one thing that depends on your device is which date counts as today.
