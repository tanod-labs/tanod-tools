How the conversions work

Each category has a base unit: metres per second for speed, pascals for pressure and metres for length. A value is multiplied by the size of its unit in the base unit and divided by the size of the target unit. All factors are exact definitions: 1 knot = 1,852/3,600 m/s, 1 mph = 1,609.344/3,600 m/s, 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 nautical mile = 1,852 m, 1 statute mile = 1,609.344 m, 1 hPa = 100 Pa, 1 mmHg = 133.322387415 Pa and 1 inHg = 25.4 mmHg = 3,386.38864 Pa (the conventional values used in ICAO Annex 5 and NIST Special Publication 811).

Temperature has different zero points, so it is converted through kelvin: K = °C + 273.15 and °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. The unit list follows ICAO Annex 5 (Units of Measurement to be Used in Air and Ground Operations), in which pressure is in hectopascals, altitude in feet or metres and speed in knots or km/h. Not for flight planning or navigation.
