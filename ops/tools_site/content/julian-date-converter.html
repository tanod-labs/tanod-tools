The formulas

A calendar date is converted to a Julian date with the algorithm in Jean Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms (2nd ed., 1998), chapter 7, and the page uses the equivalent Unix-time relation JD = Unix seconds / 86,400 + 2,440,587.5, which holds for the proleptic Gregorian calendar of your browser. The Modified Julian date is MJD = JD − 2,400,000.5 (IAU, 1973). The J2000.0 epoch is JD 2,451,545.0.

GPS time is a continuous time scale that began at 1980-01-06 00:00:00 UTC (Interface Specification IS-GPS-200). The GPS week is floor(seconds since that epoch / 604,800), the seconds of week is the remainder, and the broadcast 10-bit week is the week modulo 1,024. GPS time equals UTC plus the number of leap seconds inserted since 1980: 18 since 2017-01-01, from the table of 18 insertions published by the IERS (Bulletin C). The tool applies the count valid at the instant in question.

Leap seconds are announced by the IERS only months ahead, so GPS times for dates in the future assume no new leap second. Not for navigation.
