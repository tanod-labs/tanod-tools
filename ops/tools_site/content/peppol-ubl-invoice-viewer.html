    Peppol, UBL and EN 16931
    

    Peppol is a network for exchanging business documents between companies and public bodies, used in much of Europe and beyond. Its invoices are UBL 2.1 XML files: a standard from OASIS that the European standard EN 16931 builds on. A Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 invoice therefore has a fixed set of business terms, such as the seller's VAT identifier, the invoice lines and the VAT breakdown, which this page reads and lays out as a normal invoice.
    

    Many countries use the same structure with their own additions, for example XRechnung in Germany. If the file names its profile in the customization identifier, it is shown in the header. Since 1 January 2025 every business in Germany has to be able to receive structured e-invoices, and France requires it from 1 September 2026, so files like this reach more and more people who have never seen the XML.
    

    What is shown, and what is not
    

    You see the parties with VAT identifiers, legal and electronic addresses, delivery and payee where given, the dates and references, every line item with quantity, unit, price and VAT, discounts and charges, the VAT breakdown with exemption reasons, totals (also VAT in a second currency), payment means with IBAN, BIC and reference, notes, and attached documents. It does not validate the invoice and does not use the Peppol network.
