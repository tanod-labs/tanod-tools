Putting pages in order

Pages end up in the wrong order all the time: double-sided scans, documents assembled from several sources, a signature page that belongs at the end. Here you see every page as a thumbnail and move it where it belongs.

The new PDF is built by copying the pages in your order. Nothing on the pages is changed, and links, text and images come through intact.

Tips

  • Combining several files in a particular order? Merge PDF lets you order the files first.
  • After reordering, Add page numbers so the order is visible on paper.
