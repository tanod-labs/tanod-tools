    Making a PDF from images without uploading them

    A PDF can hold a JPG exactly as it is, which is why converting a photo to PDF doesn't have to lose quality. Your browser builds the document with pdf-lib: each image becomes one page, and JPG data is copied in without being decoded or recompressed.

    Phone photos often carry an EXIF orientation flag instead of being stored upright. PDF viewers ignore that flag, so such images are turned upright first and saved again as JPG. Images in other formats are converted by your browser to PNG, which PDF supports with full quality and transparency.

    Tips

    • Choose Fit to image for photos and scans you want to keep at their own proportions, or A4 or US Letter when the PDF will be printed.
    • To convert the other way, use PDF to JPG.
    • To add this PDF to others, use Merge PDF.
    • Mostly screenshots? PNG to PDF explains how transparency and sharp text are kept.
