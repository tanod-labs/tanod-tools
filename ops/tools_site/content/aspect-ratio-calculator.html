Aspect ratios in plain numbers

An aspect ratio is the shape of a rectangle: its width compared with its height. It says nothing about size, so 1280 × 720, 1920 × 1080 and 3840 × 2160 are all the same 16:9 shape. To reduce a size to its ratio, divide both sides by the largest number that divides them evenly.

To resize while keeping the shape, multiply or divide by the ratio: new height = new width × ratio height ÷ ratio width, and new width = new height × ratio width ÷ ratio height. If you change only one side without the other, the picture is stretched or squashed.

When the shapes don't match, for example a 4:3 photo in a 16:9 frame, you have three choices: crop the picture to the new shape, fit it inside with bars at the sides, or stretch it. Cropping or fitting keeps things looking right; stretching rarely does.

Tips

  • Change an image's pixel size with Resize image, keeping the ratio you worked out here.
  • Cut a picture to a new shape, such as square or 16:9, with Crop image.
