    Rotating and flipping images

    A quarter turn or a flip just moves pixels to new positions, so nothing is invented or lost. Turning 90 degrees swaps the width and height: a 1200 × 800 landscape becomes 800 × 1200. Flipping mirrors the picture left to right or top to bottom and keeps its size.

    The buttons stack: rotate right twice and you get a half turn, and a flip followed by a rotation is remembered as one combined change. The preview applies that change to a small copy of your first image; the saved files apply it to each full-size original.

    Tips

    • To crop away the edges after turning, use Crop image.
