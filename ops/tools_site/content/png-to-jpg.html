    When to turn a PNG into a JPG

    PNG keeps every pixel exactly, which is ideal for screenshots, logos and diagrams but wasteful for photographs: a phone photo saved as PNG can be ten times the size of the same photo as JPG. Converting such images to JPG makes them quick to email, upload to a form with a size cap, or put on a web page.

    JPG gets its small size by discarding fine detail the eye is unlikely to miss, and the quality slider decides how much is discarded. It cannot store transparency, so the tool composites the image over a solid colour first. The pixel dimensions do not change; only the way they are stored does.

    Tips

    • If the image is text, a chart or a logo, keep it as PNG, or shrink the PNG with Compress image instead.
    • Still too large? Reduce the pixel dimensions with Resize image before converting.
    • Going the other way? Use JPG to PNG.
